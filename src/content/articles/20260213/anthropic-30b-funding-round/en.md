## One of the Largest AI Funding Rounds in History

On February 12, 2026, Anthropic announced the completion of its **Series G funding round, raising $30 billion** with a **post-money valuation of $380 billion**. This makes it one of the largest venture capital deals in 2026 and the second-largest of all time.

## Deal Details

### Investor Composition

| Role | Investors |
|------|-----------|
| **Lead Investors** | GIC (Singapore Sovereign Wealth Fund), Coatue Management |
| **Major Participants** | D.E. Shaw, Dragoneer, Founders Fund, Iconiq Capital, MGX |
| **Strategic Investors** | Microsoft, Nvidia |

The participation of both Microsoft and Nvidia as strategic investors is particularly noteworthy. Nvidia's dual role as both AI infrastructure supplier and Anthropic investor highlights the deep interconnections within the AI industry ecosystem.

### Financial Performance

Anthropic's financial metrics underscore its rapid growth trajectory:

- **Annual Run-Rate Revenue**: $14 billion
- **Growth Rate**: 10x year-over-year for three consecutive years
- **Valuation Trajectory**: From approximately $18 billion at Series D (late 2023) to $380 billion — a 21x increase in just over two years

## Planned Use of Funds

The capital raised is expected to be deployed across several key areas:

1. **Accelerated Model Development** — R&D for next-generation models following Claude Opus 4.6
2. **AI Infrastructure Expansion** — Significant investment in compute resources and data centers
3. **AI Safety Research** — Scaling Anthropic's core mission of AI safety research
4. **Enterprise Expansion** — Broadening partnerships with major enterprises like Goldman Sachs

## The AI Funding Arms Race

This raise is part of the broader AI industry funding competition:

| Company | Latest Raise | Valuation |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Anthropic** | $30B (Series G) | $380B |
| **OpenAI** | ~$100B (in progress) | Undisclosed |
| **xAI** | $20B (Series E) | ~$230B |

While OpenAI is pursuing a funding round approaching $100 billion, Anthropic is rapidly closing the gap. AI startups now account for 33% of all VC investment in 2026, underscoring the sector's extraordinary momentum.

## Looking Ahead

Armed with $30 billion in fresh capital, Anthropic is poised to accelerate the rollout of Claude Cowork and intensify its push into the enterprise market. The company has recently sharpened its differentiation strategy against OpenAI through free tier feature expansions and its ad-free commitment. How far this new funding will propel that strategy remains to be seen.
